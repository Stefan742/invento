## The design in short

- **21 wooden pieces, no glue, no tools.** Two race-car side panels (1, 2), front cross piece with the band hook (3), middle cross piece (4), wheel discs (5 big ×4, 6 groove ×2, 7 small ×4), washers (8), axle hook (9), plus a long and a short 6 mm dowel (10: 82 mm, 11: 74 mm).
- **Wheels:** back wheels are three discs (Ø60, Ø54, Ø60), so the middle one makes a groove for a rubber-band tyre. Front wheels are two Ø40 discs. The discs press onto the dowel (Ø5.8 hole); the dowels turn freely in the panels (Ø6.8).
- **Motor:** a rubber band from the slot hook in piece 3 to the notched hook hub on the back axle. Roll the car back and the axle winds the band; let go and it drives the back wheels. At the end the band slips off the hub's notch, so the car coasts instead of braking.
- **Can't go together wrong:** the two cross pieces have different tab heights (14 mm and 10 mm), so each only fits its own slots. The left panel is cut mirrored, so its engraving (race number 01, INVENTO, speed lines) reads from outside.
- **Built-in science:** counting wheel turns (1 turn of the back wheel ≈ 19 cm), tyres vs no tyres, floor vs carpet.

## To check on the first prototype

- Motor band length: the hook-to-hub distance is about 108 mm. Try bands around 90–100 mm long when flat, or two small bands linked, and pick the one that pulls well without lifting the front.
- Tyre bands: small bands that sit tight in the Ø54 groove of the back wheels.
- Let 2–3 children build it from the guide and race it; note where they get stuck.
